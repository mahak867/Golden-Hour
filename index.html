For cyber-fraud victims in Tamil Nadu, and the banks holding their money

Tamil Nadu froze ₹772 crore of scam money in 2024. About ₹84 crore reached the victims.

Golden Hour works in that gap. It reads the victim's UPI screenshot, picks the official recovery routes, drafts the filings in தமிழ் and English, and gives the bank one reconciled case packet.

    Where Golden Hour acts

    1
    After the freeze, not before the scamDetection tools stop at the alert. The money is stuck between "frozen" and "returned".
    2
    One packet for every official routeMRM restoration, BNSS s.503, bank dispute and RB-IOS, pre-filled in the victim's language.
    3
    Clocks that decide the outcomeBank-report window and the hold-lift date are tracked with reminders.
    4
    Banks close cases fasterStructured packets, exact pro-rata splits and explainable mule clusters, with a human approving.

    Rule configuration

    Used by the Route Planner and Deadline Guardian. Deterministic and versioned; every value cites a source.
    Verify against official text before production
    Operating principles. Information, not legal advice · victims review and file every document personally · no fees · never holds money · never asks for OTP or PIN · a human analyst approves every bank-side action · personal data used only with DPDP consent and deleted after 30 days.
